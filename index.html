What is my Delta home worth?

Ask for a home evaluation. I look at your home and recent sales nearby and give you a number I can explain. Request an evaluation.

Which Delta neighbourhood is right for me?

It depends on how you live. Tsawwassen is a beach town by the ferry, Ladner is a village on the river dikes, and North Delta is closer to the Fraser and the bridges. I will walk you through each one.

Do you work outside Delta?

Yes. I also help clients in nearby Richmond, Surrey and Langley.

I am buying my first home. Where do I start?
